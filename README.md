# Corporate Governance and Ethics Policy – TCS

This project is a case study on the Corporate Governance and Ethics Policy of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS). It explains how a company follows honesty, transparency, accountability, and fair business practices.

It covers the main ethical rules, legal compliance, policy evaluation, business importance, and recommendations for improving corporate governance.

**Objective:** To understand how corporate governance and ethics help prevent corruption, protect stakeholders, reduce business risks, and build trust.

**Company Studied:** Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
